About WiseView

WiseView is a data viewer operated by the American Museum of Natural History. It blinks multi-epoch infrared cutouts; there is no model inference and no training pipeline. SPHEREx mode reads Level 2 spectral images from IRSA (D1–D6), unWISE mode reads W1/W2 coadds from unWISE.
